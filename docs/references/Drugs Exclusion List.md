Exclusion criteria rationale
Exclusions are set by drug class, not by a drug-by-drug judgment, so the rule can be checked against a published list.
1. Anticoagulants. A small error in dose or quality can cause serious bleeding or clotting. Warfarin and the newer anticoagulants carry boxed warnings for these risks, per FDA labels (not yet checked against current versions).
2. Transplant immunosuppressants. Levels that drop too low risk organ rejection, and levels that run too high risk toxicity. Clinicians monitor these drugs with blood tests.
What the exclusion does not do: it does not say that any other drug is safe. The app states this on every result.

Risks of the exclusion:
- Users may read an excluded drug as "no data" rather than "not assessed." The app should show a different message for each.
- The list will grow under pressure. Each addition needs the same published-criterion test.
Review: re-check the list against FDA labels every six months, and log each change with its date and source.
