- {{PRODUCT_NAME}} provides information from public FDA recall records. It does not provide medical
  advice, diagnosis or treatment.
- Using the app does not create a doctor–patient or pharmacist–patient relationship.
- **Do not stop, change or replace a prescribed medication because of this app.** Talk to your
  pharmacist or prescriber. If an official FDA recall notice contains instructions, follow the notice
  and your healthcare professional's advice.
- Not finding a recall does not mean a medication is safe or free of quality problems. FDA data can
  be incomplete or delayed.
- A "match" means a public recall record appears to apply to the details you provided. Only your
  pharmacist, prescriber or the recalling company can confirm whether your supply is affected.
- The app does not assess some medications (such as blood thinners and transplant medicines) by
  policy. This does not mean other medications are safe.
- In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number. For questions about a medication, contact
  your pharmacist, prescriber or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
- The app is an evaluation prototype and has not been clinically validated.
