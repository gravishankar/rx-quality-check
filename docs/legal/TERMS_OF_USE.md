## 2. What the app does (intended use)

The app helps you look up publicly available U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) drug recall
records (Enforcement Reports, accessed through openFDA) for a prescription medication whose details
you enter. It shows:
- whether a recall record appears to match your medication, might match it, or whether no matching
  record was identified in the sources it could check;
- where the information came from, when it was retrieved, and what could not be determined.

The app is for **information only**. It is an evaluation prototype that has **not been clinically
validated**.

## 3. What the app does not do

- It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not create a doctor–patient
  or pharmacist–patient relationship.
- It does not tell you whether to take, stop, change or replace a medication. Do not stop, change or
  replace a prescribed medication because of an app result. Talk to your pharmacist or prescriber.
- It does not determine whether a medication is safe, effective or free of quality problems. Not
  finding a recall does not mean a medication is safe.
- It does not monitor your medications or notify you of new recalls. It checks only when you ask.
- It does not assess some medications by design (see the in-app list of excluded medications).
- It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by FDA, the National Library of Medicine or any
  manufacturer, labeler or pharmacy.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- Enter medication details carefully. Results depend on what you enter and on public data.
- Use the app as one source of information, not the only basis for any medication decision.
- For any recall that matches or might match, contact your pharmacist or healthcare professional and
  read the official FDA record.
- Keep your phone secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone may see saved information.

## 5. Data sources and limitations

The app relies on public data from FDA (openFDA) and the National Library of Medicine (RxNorm,
DailyMed). These sources can be incomplete, delayed, inconsistent or wrong, and we do not control
them. See "Data Sources and Limitations".

## 6. Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: medication information stays on your phone; only public drug codes
